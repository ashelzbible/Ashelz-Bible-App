## Full description (4000 characters or fewer)
Ashelz Bible App is a free Bible and devotional app made to put the Word within reach of everyone, everywhere.

READ
- The Bible in many versions and languages, with the King James Version working offline.
- Tap verses to copy, share, highlight, or save them to your notes.
- Search by book or type a reference such as John 3 or Psalms 23:1.

GROW
- A daily devotional with a verse, a short reading, a prayer, and a longer teaching when you want to go deeper.
- Messages of Hope to swipe through, save, and share.
- Deep Topics and Prayers, and a Counseling Portal with scripture, guidance, and a Pray Now button.
- A free library of Christian books to read and download.

PRAY AND REFLECT
- My Prayers with a prayer streak, a journal, and notes you can write like a document.
- A gentle daily reminder, if you turn it on.

FELLOWSHIP
- Online Homecell: meet your cell group face to face with video and sound, wherever you are.

MADE WITH CARE
- 100% free. No ads. No subscriptions. No paywalls.
- Your prayers, notes, and journal stay on your own phone.
- Choose your accent colour, dark mode, and text size.

Founded and developed by Loren-Ashlyn De Koe, Windhoek, Namibia. Dedicated to making the Word available to everyone, everywhere, entirely for free.

Bible versions come from public domain collections and from services that supply licensed text. Each version shows its own copyright terms.
