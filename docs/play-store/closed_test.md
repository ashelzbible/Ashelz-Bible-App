# Closed test kit

New personal Google Play developer accounts must run a closed test before applying for production. The rule when this was written was **12 testers who stay opted in for 14 days in a row**. Check the current numbers in Play Console, Testing, Closed testing, because Google changes them.

## Steps
1. Build the Android package and upload it to a **Closed testing** track (see android_wrapper.md).
2. Make a testers list: add the Gmail addresses of at least 12 people (aim for 15 to 20 in case some drop out).
3. Send each tester the opt in link that Play Console shows, with the message below.
4. Testers must **accept the invite and install the app from Play**, and keep it installed for the whole period.
5. Day 14: in Play Console, Dashboard, Apply for production. Answer the questions about your test honestly.

## Message to send to testers
Hello, I would love your help to test the Ashelz Bible App before it goes on Google Play. Please:
1. Open this link on your Android phone and tap "Become a tester": [paste the opt in link]
2. Install the app from the Play Store page that opens.
3. Open the app every few days for the next two weeks and look around: read a chapter, open the daily devotion, and write a prayer.
4. Please do not uninstall it until I tell you the test is over. If you see anything wrong, write to ashlyn.dekoe1@gmail.com.
Thank you, and God bless.
